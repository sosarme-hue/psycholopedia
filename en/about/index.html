Who is behind it

Psycholopedia grows out of clinical experience and curiosity about the human mind.

Alejandro Rosàs Navarro

I am a family doctor, specialising in Preventive Medicine and Public Health, with more than 38 years of experience in primary care, registered with the Barcelona Medical Association (Col·legi Oficial de Metges de Barcelona), licence no. 28574. In the surgery I have learned that many of the questions people bring are not only medical: they have to do with how we remember, how we worry, how we relate to others and how we change over our lives.

Psycholopedia aims to make psychology accessible to everyone: its history, its people, its methods and what it can offer everyday life, with references and acknowledging what is still unknown.

Collaborators

For psychological assessment, psychometrics and specific clinical content, Psycholopedia will include review by psychology professionals with accredited expertise. If you would like to collaborate, write to us.

What it is and is not

Psycholopedia is a popular encyclopedia. It does not offer diagnosis, treatment or personal consultation, and it does not replace care from health professionals.

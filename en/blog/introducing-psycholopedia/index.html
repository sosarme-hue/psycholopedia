Understand the mind, understand life: introducing Psycholopedia

Why an encyclopedia of psychology that links the discipline's history with everyday questions.

In primary care, many questions are not strictly medical: why can't I sleep since my mother died, why has my teenage son stopped telling me anything, why do I leave everything until the last minute. Behind each one lie decades of psychological research that seldom reach the public clearly.

Psycholopedia aims to fill that gap. It has three ways in:

  • Discover: the history of psychology, its people, schools and experiments, including the women and contexts the textbooks left out.
  • Understand: the major areas, from memory to personality, and how they apply to everyday situations.
  • Take part: quizzes, reasoning challenges, reflection activities and courses.

We will try to follow three rules: cite our sources, keep what is historically important apart from what has scientific support today, and never turn anyone into a label.

This blog is updated every three days with three short articles: stories, experiments, everyday questions or reflections from the surgery. Welcome.

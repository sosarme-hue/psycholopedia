Psychology in film, literature and music

The song that will not leave your head

Nine in ten people get "earworms" at least once a week. There are tricks to get rid of them.

A catchy chorus that repeats over and over without you choosing it. In German it is called Ohrwurm, "ear worm", which gave rise to the English earworm. Surveys in Finland and the UK found that about 90% of people get them at least once a week.

Which songs stick

A 2016 study analysed the most frequently named songs: they tend to have a fast tempo, a simple melody and some unexpected leap in the intervals. They stick more if we have heard them recently, listen to them often or something reminds us of them.

How do you get rid of one? Some strategies with a degree of support are listening to the whole song, since what is left unfinished repeats more; chewing gum, which occupies the systems we use for "singing" in our heads; or doing a moderately demanding task such as a crossword or an anagram.

Myths that sound scientific

Do women talk more than men?

It was claimed that women say 20,000 words a day and men 7,000. When it was measured, the difference vanished.

In 2006, a popular book claimed that women speak about 20,000 words a day and men about 7,000. The figure was quoted around the world, but it did not come from any study, and the author removed it from later editions.

Recording everything

In 2007, Matthias Mehl and colleagues asked almost 400 students in the United States and Mexico to wear a small recorder that automatically captured a few seconds of sound every few minutes for several days. From those samples they estimated how many words each person spoke.

Women said about 16,000 words a day and men about 15,700. The difference was not significant. What did vary a lot was the difference between individuals: the chattiest of both sexes exceeded 45,000 words a day, and the quietest said fewer than 1,000.

References

  1. Mehl, M. R. et al. (2007). Are women really more talkative than men? Science, 317(5834), 82.

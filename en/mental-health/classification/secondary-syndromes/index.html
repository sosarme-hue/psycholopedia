Secondary syndromes and psychological factors affecting other conditions

CIE-11: block 6E40 · 6E60–6E6Z · look up in ICD-11 ↗
DSM-5-TR: Disorders due to another medical condition · Psychological factors affecting other medical conditions

Some psychological symptoms are directly caused by a physical illness: a thyroid problem can produce anxiety or depression; a brain injury, personality changes; encephalitis, psychotic symptoms. That is why, when a new condition appears, the doctor also assesses physical health and, if needed, orders tests. These syndromes improve when the cause is treated.

The reverse path also exists: psychological factors, such as stress or not following treatment, can worsen illnesses such as diabetes or hypertension. Both classifications have sections for these situations, a reminder that body and mind cannot be separated.

What it includes

  • Psychotic, mood, anxiety, personality and other syndromes secondary to a medical condition (for example thyroid, neurological or autoimmune)
  • Psychological or behavioural factors affecting disorders classified elsewhere

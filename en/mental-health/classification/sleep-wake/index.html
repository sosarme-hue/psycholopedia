Sleep-wake disorders

CIE-11: block 7A00–7B2Z (capítulo 7) · look up in ICD-11 ↗
DSM-5-TR: Sleep-wake disorders

ICD-11 has moved sleep disorders out of the mental disorders chapter and given them a chapter of their own, chapter 7, because many have respiratory, neurological or circadian causes. DSM-5-TR keeps them as a chapter within the manual.

Chronic insomnia is the most common and has an effective psychological treatment, cognitive behavioural therapy for insomnia. Others, such as sleep apnoea or narcolepsy, require investigation in a sleep unit. Poor sleep and mental health influence each other in both directions.

What it includes

  • Insomnia
  • Hypersomnolence disorders, such as narcolepsy
  • Sleep-related breathing disorders, such as apnoea
  • Circadian rhythm disorders
  • Sleep-related movement disorders, such as restless legs
  • Parasomnias: nightmares, sleepwalking, night terrors, REM sleep behaviour disorder

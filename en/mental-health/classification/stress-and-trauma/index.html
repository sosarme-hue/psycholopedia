Disorders specifically associated with stress

CIE-11: block 6B40–6B4Z · look up in ICD-11 ↗
DSM-5-TR: Trauma- and stressor-related disorders

They are the only disorders that, by definition, require a triggering event: a trauma, a loss or a stressful life change. Post-traumatic stress involves reliving what happened, avoiding reminders and feeling constantly on alert. Adjustment disorder is greater distress than expected in response to a difficult change.

ICD-11 includes complex post-traumatic stress disorder, typical of prolonged trauma such as childhood abuse or ongoing violence, which adds difficulties regulating emotions, a negative self-image and relationship problems. DSM-5-TR does not list it as a separate diagnosis. Both now include prolonged grief, for grief that, long after the loss, still prevents the person from living.

What it includes

  • Post-traumatic stress disorder
  • Complex post-traumatic stress disorder
  • Prolonged grief disorder
  • Adjustment disorder
  • Reactive attachment disorder
  • Disinhibited social engagement disorder

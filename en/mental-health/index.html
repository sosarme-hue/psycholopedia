Mental health with rigour and without labels

A broad section, with specialist medical and psychological review before each dossier is published.

Need help now? In Spain, 024 is free and open 24 hours for people with suicidal thoughts and those close to them. In an emergency call 112. Elsewhere, contact your local helpline or emergency services. Your family doctor is also a good place to start.

Emotions, difficulties and disorders

Feeling sad, afraid or worried is part of life. We speak of a difficulty when something is harder than usual for a while. The World Health Organization describes a mental disorder as a clinically significant disturbance in cognition, emotional regulation or behaviour, usually associated with distress or impairment in important areas of functioning. Telling these levels apart avoids both downplaying what matters and turning into illness what is not.

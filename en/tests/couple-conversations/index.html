Couple reflection

Couple conversations

Seven areas with questions to discuss calmly. There is no score and no "87% compatible": the aim is to discover what is worth talking about.

You can each answer separately and then compare, or read the questions together. Pick one or two areas per conversation; there is no need to do it all at once.

1. Listening and communication
  • When do you feel I really listen to you?
  • Which topic do we find hardest to discuss calmly?
  • What gesture of mine helps you when you're worried?
2. Agreements and disagreements
  • Which disagreement can we actually leave as it is?
  • How would we like to ask for a break when an argument heats up?
  • Which recent agreement has worked well?
3. Trust and autonomy
  • What space of your own do you need to feel well?
  • What makes you feel trust in our relationship?
  • Is there something you'd like to do without me?
4. Sharing responsibilities
  • Which tasks fall more on one of us?
  • What invisible mental load (remembering, planning) is there?
  • What would we change in how we share things this month?
5. Affection and intimacy
  • How do you like me to show affection?
  • Which recent moment made us feel close?
  • What do we need to have more unhurried time together?
6. Expectations and plans
  • Where do we picture ourselves in five years?
  • Which personal project of yours would you like me to support?
  • What expectation do you have that you may not have told me?
7. Family transitions
  • Which change (moving, children, work, caregiving) lies ahead?
  • What did we learn from a previous change?
  • What outside help would be useful?
Fear, violence or control are not everyday disagreements and cannot be fixed by a conversation. If you feel in danger or controlled, seek specialist help. In Spain: 016 (free, does not appear on the bill) or 112 in an emergency.

Tests

Free-to-use tests to take and download

12 short questionnaires whose authors allow free reproduction. You can answer them here, with an approximate score, or download them as a printable PDF.

They guide, they do not diagnose. These are screening and self-assessment questionnaires used in primary care and research. A high score means it is worth talking to a professional; a low score does not rule out a problem if you feel something is wrong. Your answers are scored in your browser and are neither stored nor sent.

Why are the Beck, HADS or STAI not here?

Because they are copyrighted and can only be used under licence from their publishers. You will find them described, without their items, in the instrument catalogue.

For clinical or research use outside English-speaking settings, use the validated adaptation for your language.

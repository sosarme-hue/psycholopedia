RSES · 2 min

Rosenberg Self-Esteem Scale

Global self-esteem.

A screening questionnaire: it guides, it does not diagnose. Your answers are scored in your browser and are neither stored nor sent.

Download printable PDF

Indicate how strongly you agree or disagree with each statement.

1. On the whole, I am satisfied with myself
2. At times I think I am no good at all
3. I feel that I have a number of good qualities
4. I am able to do things as well as most other people
5. I feel I do not have much to be proud of
6. I certainly feel useless at times
7. I feel that I'm a person of worth, at least on an equal plane with others
8. I wish I could have more respect for myself
9. All in all, I am inclined to feel that I am a failure
10. I take a positive attitude toward myself

Score from 0 to 30, following Rosenberg's original scoring. Higher scores indicate higher self-esteem. It is not a clinical measure.

About this questionnaire

Authors: Morris Rosenberg (1965).

Licence: Free to use: Morris Rosenberg's family permits its use for research and education without asking permission, provided the source is cited.

Scoring: 0–14: Low self-esteem; 15–25: Self-esteem in the usual range; 26–30: High self-esteem.

References

  1. Rosenberg M. Society and the Adolescent Self-Image. Princeton: Princeton University Press; 1965.

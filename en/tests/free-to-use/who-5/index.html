WHO-5 Well-Being Index

Psychological well-being over the past two weeks.

A screening questionnaire: it guides, it does not diagnose. Your answers are scored in your browser and are neither stored nor sent.

Download printable PDF

Please indicate for each of the five statements which is closest to how you have been feeling over the last two weeks.

1. I have felt cheerful and in good spirits
2. I have felt calm and relaxed
3. I have felt active and vigorous
4. I woke up feeling fresh and rested
5. My daily life has been filled with things that interest me

The raw score (0–25) is multiplied by 4 to give a percentage from 0 to 100. Scores of 50 or below suggest having your mood assessed by a professional.

About this questionnaire

Authors: Organización Mundial de la Salud (1998).

Licence: © Psychiatric Research Unit, Mental Health Centre North Zealand. Free to use without permission.

Scoring: 0–28: Very low well-being; 29–50: Low well-being; 51–100: Well-being in the usual range.

References

  1. Topp CW, Østergaard SD, Søndergaard S, Bech P. The WHO-5 Well-Being Index: a systematic review of the literature. Psychother Psychosom. 2015;84(3):167-176.
